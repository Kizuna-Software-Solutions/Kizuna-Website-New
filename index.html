Project Accelerate: AI Driven Development

The Hypothesis

Traditional software development cycles spend up to 50% of their timelines on boilerplate code, repetitive CRUD operations, and manual environment setups. We hypothesized that by building a specialized pipeline using secure, fine-tuned AI development models, we could automate these baseline tasks—freeing our engineers to focus 100% of their cognitive energy on complex architecture, custom business logic, and security.

The Implementation

We engineered an internal AI driven workflow within Kizuna. When a new project kicks off:

  1. 01 AI Synthesis: Custom AI tools instantly scaffold the database schemas, generate initial API endpoints, and establish robust test coverage based on the product requirements.
  2. 02 Human Engineering: Our senior software engineers step in immediately as architects and code editors. They review, optimize, secure, and integrate the code to enterprise-ready standards.

Which AI approach is right for your project? Here's how we think about it.

Fine-tuned Model
Best for: Domain expertise

When you need a model that deeply understands your industry vocabulary, documents, or processes. High accuracy, higher upfront cost.

e.g. Legal contract analysis, medical imaging
RAG Pipeline
Best for: Knowledge retrieval

When your data changes frequently and you need the AI to always answer from up-to-date sources. Fast to deploy, easy to update.

e.g. Internal knowledge base, support bots
Agentic Pipeline
Best for: Multi-step automation

When tasks require reasoning across multiple steps, tools, or APIs autonomously. Ideal for complex workflows with minimal human intervention.

e.g. Automated research, data pipelines
Off-the-shelf API
Best for: Speed to market

When you need AI capabilities fast without custom training. Lower cost, quick integration, ideal for standard tasks with proven models.

e.g. Sentiment analysis, translation, OCR
